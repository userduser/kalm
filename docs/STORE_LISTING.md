## Detailed description

Kalm blocks ads and helps hide scam/NSFW comment bots on YouTube.

Ad blocking

- Block ads and trackers across websites, with filters for YouTube ads.
- Choose Off, Basic, Optimal or Complete filtering for each site.
- Create a custom element filter or remove an unwanted element.
- Optional blocked request counts on the toolbar where supported.

Comment bot blocker

- Screen comments before they appear on videos and Shorts.
- Hide comments with blocked links in the text or the author's public channel bio.
- Filter authors with visible channel-promotion shelves, including accounts whose
  comment text looks ordinary.
- Add your own sites and literal words or phrases to one blocklist.
- Optionally block every comment containing a link, or channel links and mentions.
- Turn comment protection on or off independently of ad blocking.

Checks run in your browser. Author decisions are cached on your device to reduce
repeat requests. Kalm has no account, analytics or hosted comment-screening service.

Kalm filters links, keywords and channel information; it does not analyse profile
pictures. The channel-promotion rule can hide legitimate accounts too. New author
checks can delay comments, and changes to YouTube can affect filtering.

Free and open source: https://github.com/userduser/kalm

The ad-blocking engine is based on uBlock Origin Lite. Kalm is an independent
project distributed under GPL-3.0-or-later, and is not affiliated with YouTube,
Google or the upstream project.
